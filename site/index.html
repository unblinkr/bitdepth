How does this endpoint compare, and what does the grade mean?

Measured quality

A grade from A to D for every endpoint, from the output it returned against the reference, not the label it declared. All four columns print with the letter, and the noise floor prints beside them, so a difference smaller than the endpoint's own night-to-night variation is never called a difference.

Which cheaper endpoints meet my bar?

Price at grade

For each model, the lowest listed price among the endpoints holding A, and holding B, with the date the price was read.

What happens when an endpoint changes?

Nightly monitoring

Every endpoint, every night. A grade that changes is a dated event, reported the next morning with the column that moved. You hear about it before your users do.

How it works

Probe, reference, grade.

1

Probe

120 fixed prompts and 24 generated that night from a recorded seed, in five families (arithmetic, JSON, string puzzles, a planted code in about 2,500 words, exact repetition), go to every endpoint of a model, pinned to one provider, fallbacks off, temperature 0.

model-120b · 25 endpoints · 144 prompts each
pinned provider · allow_fallbacks: false · temp 0 · seed 42
2

Reference

Each output is compared word for word against the plurality of endpoints declaring full precision. Each family also has its own answer key, so task accuracy is scored on its own. Each endpoint is compared with itself night to night, which gives the noise floor.

reference: plurality of bf16/fp16 endpoints
answer keys: per family · self-agreement: per endpoint
3

Grade

A to D on four columns: match, task accuracy, declared precision held, reliability. The lowest column wins and the reason prints with the letter. U until seven nights exist. Letters are provisional until the first calibration publishes, then final under that version of the method.

endpoint 07 · M 97.2 (floor 4.0) · T -0.7 · D held · R 0.4%
grade A · 7 of 7 nights · method v0

What a grade means

And what it doesn't.

A grade measures defined behaviors against a stated reference: did the endpoint return what the full-precision endpoints returned, did it answer the probes correctly against their answer keys, did its declared precision stay the same, did it respond. It does not rank models, it does not say an endpoint is best, and measured behavior is not proof of the weights or precision an endpoint runs. Declared precision is recorded as a claim and held to consistency over time. Thresholds, the reference rule, the noise rule, a worked example and the limits are on the method page.

For providers

Your endpoint is already in the comparison. Your team sees its results first and can flag factual errors before publication; publication then follows the same rules for every endpoint, whatever the grade. The policy is on the method page.
