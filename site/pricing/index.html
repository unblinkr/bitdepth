Questions

How Watch works.

What counts as an endpoint?

One provider serving one model at one declared precision, for example deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro:atlas-cloud/fp8. The tags are in the public table, in each model's JSON, and in get_scorecard.

How do I pick them?

Tick them on the Watch page, from the list of every endpoint we grade. The price follows the count. Reply to any Watch email to change the list.

What is a change?

The endpoint moves inside or outside the noise floor, its declared precision changes, its listed price moves, or it appears or leaves the router. The rules are on the method page.

What emails do I get?

A welcome email within the hour, with where each endpoint stands. A status email every Monday on all of them. And an alert the morning after any of them changes, usually early US Eastern.

Numbers against a stated reference. Not a claim about intent.
