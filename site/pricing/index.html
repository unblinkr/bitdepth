Questions

How Watch works.

What counts as an endpoint?

One provider serving one model at one declared precision, for example deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro:atlas-cloud/fp8. The tags are in the public table and in each model's JSON.

How do I pick them?

At checkout, list the endpoints you route to, separated by commas. Set the quantity to how many you listed. Write to hello@bitdepth.dev to change the list.

What is a change?

The endpoint moves inside or outside the noise floor, its declared precision changes, its listed price moves, or it appears or leaves the router. The rules are on the method page.

When does the email arrive?

After the nightly run finishes, usually early morning US Eastern. Nothing arrives on a night with no change.
