How a block works

1

You choose the model, the grade you need, and your monthly volume.

2

We match you with a provider whose endpoint holds that grade.

3

You pick the shape: a fixed price per million output tokens for 90 days, or the provider's market price with the grade guaranteed.

4

Bitdepth grades it nightly. You and the provider see the same result each morning.

Two shapes

Lock the price, or lock the grade.

Same grade condition, same credits, same right to hand on the remainder.

Fixed price

A price per million output tokens that does not move for 90 days. For a buyer with flat revenue and a per-token bill. You carry the risk that list prices fall; you get a cost you can plan on.

Market price, grade guaranteed

You pay the provider's listed price as it moves. For a buyer who expects prices to keep falling and wants the guarantee, not the lock. The nightly grade, the credits and the exit are the same.

For providers

Sell capacity at a committed price to a buyer who knows exactly what they're getting. A good grade is your proof, checked every night by someone who isn't you. Already sell reserved capacity? Keep it. A block adds the grade and gives your buyer an exit.

What this is not

Not a place to resell credits or keys. Every lab's terms forbid that, and we won't touch it. A block is a claim the provider issues, so handing it on is a reissue by the provider, not a key swap.

How the grade stays fair

Bitdepth runs the market and never serves inference itself. Every grade is computed by code from a published method, the same for every endpoint, and paying Bitdepth for anything can't change a letter.
