Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026. Bitdepth is run by Autumn Sun LLC.

The short version

Bitdepth publishes grades of public AI endpoints. The site, the free JSON at /v1 and the MCP server need no account and no sign-in. We do not ask who you are to read them.

What we collect

  • Requests to the site, API and MCP server. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard request logs (IP address, time, path, user agent) for a short period to run and secure the service. We use them for debugging and abuse prevention only.
  • MCP tool calls. The MCP server receives the tool name and its arguments, such as a model or endpoint name. It does not receive your conversation, and we do not store tool arguments beyond those request logs.
  • Watch subscribers. If you subscribe, Stripe handles payment and gives us your email address and the endpoints you chose, so we can send your nightly emails and issue your API key. We never see your card number.
  • Pay per call. x402 payments settle on the Base network, which is public by design. We see the paying wallet address and the amount.
  • Email. If you write to hello@bitdepth.dev, we keep the thread to reply to you.

What we do not do

No analytics trackers, no advertising cookies, no sale or rental of personal data.

Who else sees it

Vercel (hosting), Stripe (payments), Resend (email delivery) and the Base network (x402 payments), each only for what it does for us.

How long we keep it

Request logs follow Vercel's retention. Subscriber records stay while the subscription is active and up to 12 months after, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to hello@bitdepth.dev. We answer within 30 days.
